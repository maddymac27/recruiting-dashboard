<objective>
Surface ingestion health at a glance (REL-03) and make the REL-04 silent-recall gap a visible, permanent open risk (REL-04). Extend the sidebar Ingestion Health block with a success/failure last-sync line (failure persists in destructive color until the next success), Review/Dead-letter count badges that link to their queues (dead-letter badge turns red when > 0), and a non-dismissible neutral risk note with the locked REL-04 copy. Data is read server-side in layout (the single dashboardMode reader) and passed down as props.

Vertical-slice value: after this plan the user can tell — without opening anything — whether the last sync worked, how much is waiting in each queue, and that unlisted-sender mail can still be missed. This completes the fail-loud surfacing loop and closes the phase.

Output: extended `ingestion-health.tsx`, layout data plumbing, and a visual verification.
</objective>
