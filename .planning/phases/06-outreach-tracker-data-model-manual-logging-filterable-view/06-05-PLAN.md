## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker running an active search, **I want to** manually log my cold outreach and see it in a filterable list — and jump to a contact's outreach — **so that** I can tell which messaging converts, with a demo-safe version to show as a portfolio piece.

**This plan (06-05) delivers the integration + demonstrability half of the slice:** the Contact-Database cross-link into the Outreach list (D-11) and the portfolio-safe demo seed (success criterion 1). It runs in parallel with 06-03 (disjoint files).

Purpose: D-11 (separate-but-linked tabs via contactId) and the demo/real-separation half of success criterion 1. Supports OUT-03 (the cross-link routes into the filterable list) and OUT-01 (seeded stored rows).
Output: extended src/domain/contacts.ts + src/components/contacts-table.tsx, new DemoOutreachFixture + fixtures in src/demo/seed/companies.ts, outreach replay in src/demo/seed/seed.ts.
</objective>
